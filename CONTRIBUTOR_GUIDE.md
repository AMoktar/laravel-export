# Contributor Quick Guide

## Periodic Tasks
- **Sync your fork:**
  - Pull changes from upstream `main` into your fork’s `main`.
- **Update your dev branch:**
  - Merge upstream `main` and feature/fix branches into your personal dev branch (e.g., `dev-moktar`).
- **Clean up branches:**
  - Delete merged feature/fix branches locally and remotely.

## Important Notes
- Never commit directly to your fork’s `main`.
- Use feature branches for PRs and a personal dev branch for your own work.
- Update Composer in your consuming project to use your dev branch.
- Always run tests before pushing changes.
- Communicate with maintainers before making major changes.

Happy contributing!
